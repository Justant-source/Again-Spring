## 3. Mechanism — Plaza Classification

### Plaza Classifier (NEW)

**File**: `ai-user/learning/app/services/plaza_classifier.py`  
**Function**: `classify_plaza(content: str, title: str) -> str`  
**Logic**:
- 6-plaza keyword sets: COUPLE (lovetalk), WORK (job/career/직장/경력), FRIEND (관계/친구/우정), FAMILY (가족/부모), MONEY (돈/재정/금융), OTHER
- Scoring: title keywords × 2, content keywords × 1 (title semantically stronger indicator)
- Output: argmax plaza name or OTHER if no keywords match
- Behavior: conservative (null/empty → OTHER stays)

**Validation**: 12/12 dry-run tests pass (2026-06-22 session).

### Crawl.py Integration — BUILT then REVERTED

A wiring (classify non-plaza categories at ingest) was prototyped, then **reverted**. Reason: the live natepan crawl's `OTHER` bucket is viral/idol/stock/news content (§4); applying `classify_plaza` at ingest would mislabel that noise INTO the plazas on every daily crawl — an active regression. The classifier remains a standalone, **unwired** module (useful only if a genuine conflict-dense, robots-clean source is found later).

## 4. Dry-Run Finding — Backfill ABORTED (empirical)

Two read-only dry-runs classified 800-row samples of the candidate buckets (no DB writes). Result: **the existing OTHER/freeboard buckets are NOT interpersonal-conflict content** — keyword reclassification produces noise.

### Broad classifier (single keyword hit → reclassify)

| Sample | Reclassified out of OTHER | Spot-check quality |
|---|---|---|
| natepan OTHER (800) | 380 / 800 (47.5%) | **Garbage**: 골반/idol body → FRIEND; 반도체 주식 → FRIEND; 신곡 뮤비 조회수 → WORK |
| clien freeboard (800) | 574 / 800 (71.8%) | **Garbage**: tech/news; FAMILY 27%·WORK 18% are incidental keyword hits |

### Strict gate (≥2 keyword hits + conflict/emotion marker)

| Sample | Kept (quality-gated) | Spot-check of KEPT |
|---|---|---|
| natepan OTHER | 13 / 800 (1.6%) | Still off-target: celebrity news, song lyrics, consumer complaint |
| clien freeboard | 46 / 800 (5.8%) | **Politics**: "민주당 지지", "장동혁 지선 패배" — not WORK/FRIEND |

**Root cause**: natepan `OTHER` = 베스트 ranking = viral/idol/stock/news; clien `park` = IT/politics/news. Neither is relationship/work-conflict-dense. Keyword presence ≠ conflict topic. Existing GOOD plaza rows (MARRIED 539, COUPLE 363) came from natepan's inherently-relationship sections (lovetalk), which `OTHER` is not.

**Decision**: ⛔ Backfill NOT run. Corpus left UNCHANGED. crawl.py wiring REVERTED (would pollute the live natepan daily crawl). `plaza_classifier.py` kept as standalone unwired infra.
